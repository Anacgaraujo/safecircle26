Text messages

Last updated November 19, 2026

Bulldog Ant sends transactional safety texts for its SafeCircle app — never marketing. Program name: Bulldog Ant SafeCircle Safety Alerts.

How people opt in

There is no website form or keyword: every number is added inside the SafeCircle mobile app.

  1. Circle members and emergency contacts. A SafeCircle user adds someone they know (a family member or friend) on the "Add participants" or "Add emergency contact" screen. Before that person can receive any text, the user must first ask them and then tick this box, which is unticked by default:
    They agreed to get SafeCircle texts at this number (invitations and safety alerts). Msg & data rates may apply. They can reply STOP to opt out.
    If the box is not ticked, our servers never text that number. The first text the person receives repeats who added them and how to opt out.
  2. SafeCircle users themselves. A user who adds their own phone number on their profile sees this notice next to the field:
    By adding your number, you agree to get SafeCircle texts about your circles' safety alerts. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

What we send

Message frequency varies with use: typically a one-time invitation or notice, and safety alerts only when someone in your circle needs help.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any SafeCircle text to stop all texts to your number. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@thebulldogant.com. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
