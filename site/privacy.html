Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy explains what the SafeCircle app ("SafeCircle", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and, if you add it, your phone number and photo.
  • People you add: names, phone numbers and email addresses of the circle members and emergency contacts you choose, and whether they agreed to receive text messages.
  • Journeys and check-ins: planned trips, check-in times and whether you confirmed you are OK.
  • Location: your location while you share it with your circles or during an active journey or alert. You can pause sharing at any time.
  • Alerts: SOS, panic and missed check-in alerts, with the time and location they were sent.

How we use it

Only to run the safety features you use: showing your location and journeys to the circles you pick, reminding you to check in, and alerting your circles and emergency contacts in the app and by text message when you send an SOS, use your panic PIN or miss a check-in. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

Only the circles and emergency contacts you choose. We use Google Firebase to store data and run the app, and Twilio to deliver text messages. These providers process data only on our behalf.

Text messages (SMS)

We text a phone number only after the person agreed to receive SafeCircle texts at that number, and only for circle invitations, emergency contact notices and safety alerts. We never send marketing texts. See how people opt in.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Reply STOP to any SafeCircle text to stop receiving them, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Your control

  • Pause location sharing or end a journey at any time.
  • Edit or remove circle members and emergency contacts.
  • Delete past journeys, or delete your account to permanently erase your circles, journeys and alerts.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted by account. Your safety and panic PINs are stored only as one-way hashes.

Children

SafeCircle is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if this policy changes. Questions: hello@thebulldogant.com.
