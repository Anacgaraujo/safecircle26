Terms of Service

Last updated November 19, 2026

1. The service

SafeCircle helps you share planned journeys, check-ins and your location with people you trust, and alert them when something is wrong. It complements — never replaces — emergency services. In a life-threatening situation, always contact local emergency services first.

2. Your responsibilities

Keep your account, safety PIN and panic PIN private. Only add people who know you and have agreed to be in your circle or to be your emergency contact, and only tick "they agreed to get SafeCircle texts" when they actually agreed.

3. Alerts and text messages

Alerts depend on your device's connectivity, battery and location permissions, and on mobile carriers. We cannot guarantee that every alert or text is delivered. Text message terms are on the text messages page.

4. Acceptable use

Do not use SafeCircle to track anyone without their knowledge, to harass anyone, or to send texts to people who have not agreed to receive them.

5. Changes

We may update these terms; continued use means you accept the updated terms.
